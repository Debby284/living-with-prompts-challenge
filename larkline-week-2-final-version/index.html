Questions

Your questions, answered at the kitchen table

The questions we hear most, in plain words. Not here? Send us a WhatsApp and your agent answers.

Ask your question

  • Do I always get the same agent?

    Yes. One agent stays with you from the first viewing to the notary. You get their own mobile number and WhatsApp.

  • How do I hear about new homes first?

    Sign up for WhatsApp alerts. You get a message before a home appears on the big portals. Free, and you can stop at any time.

  • Can you help me buy a home you do not sell?

    Yes. Your agent can guide you as a buying agent, check the price and the house, and negotiate for you.

  • Can I view a home in the evening or on Saturday?

    Often, yes. Ask your agent. We plan viewings around your working day, not ours.

  • What should I arrange before I make an offer?

    Know your budget. A talk with a mortgage advisor first makes your offer stronger and saves you stress later.

  • Do you also rent out homes?

    No. We only help people buy and sell homes. That is all we do, so we can do it well.

  • Can you tell me about a street before I buy?

    Yes. Our agents live here. They tell you honestly about noise, schools, traffic and the neighbours.

  • How long does buying take?

    From an accepted offer to the notary usually takes two to three months. Your agent keeps you up to date at every step.

  • Can I call outside office hours?

    You can always send a WhatsApp. Your agent answers as soon as they can, also in the evening.

  • What does a valuation cost?

    Nothing. The agent for your village comes by, looks at your home and the street, and gives you an honest price. No obligation.

  • Who sells my home?

    The same agent who valued it. You will not be handed to a colleague you have never met.

  • How do you set the asking price?

    We look at recent sales in your street and village, the state of your home and the market today. Then we explain the price to you.

  • Who takes the photos?

    We arrange a professional photographer, so your home looks its best online.

  • Do I need to be home for viewings?

    No. Your agent leads every viewing and tells you afterwards what people said.

  • How fast will my home sell?

    That depends on the village and the price. Your agent gives you an honest estimate at the kitchen table.

  • Can I sell before I have found a new home?

    Yes. Your agent can help you plan both, so you do not end up with two mortgages or no home at all.

  • What happens to my request?

    It is logged, so it will not get lost in an inbox. Your agent calls you to plan a visit.

  • Do you sell homes outside Horst aan de Maas?

    We focus on the sixteen villages of our municipality. That is where we know every street.

  • Which village suits me best?

    Tell us how you live and work. Your agent will tell you honestly where you would feel at home.

  • Where are the shops and the market?

    In Horst. The centre has shops, the market and café terraces within walking distance.

  • Which villages are close to the Maas?

    Broekhuizen, Broekhuizenvorst, Lottum and Grubbenvorst lie near the river.

  • Where is it quiet and green?

    Griendtsveen, America and Kronenberg, with the nature of the Peel close by.

  • Which village is quick to Venlo?

    Grubbenvorst and Sevenum. From there you are in Venlo in a few minutes.

  • Are there schools in every village?

    Most villages have a primary school. Secondary schools are in Horst and Venlo.

  • How many villages are there?

    Sixteen. Each one has its own pace and its own character.

  • Is there public transport?

    Horst-Sevenum has a train station. Buses connect most villages with Horst and Venlo.

  • Can I visit the office first?

    Yes. The coffee is on us, no appointment needed. Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00.
